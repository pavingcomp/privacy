Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how the CompactorTracker application collects, uses, and protects user data.

1. Data Collection

The application may collect the following information:

  • Location data required for compaction tracking
  • Device information (Android version, model)
  • Usage data related to app performance

2. Purpose of Data Use

Collected data is used only for:

  • Providing core application functionality
  • Improving accuracy of compaction reporting
  • Enhancing app stability and performance

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. Data may be processed by trusted services such as Firebase strictly for app functionality.

4. User Rights

Users may request deletion of their data by contacting the developer at: pavingcomp@gmail.com.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data from unauthorized access.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as needed. Updates will be published on this page.
